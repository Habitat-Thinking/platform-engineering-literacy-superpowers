# Scoring and Interpretation

## How to determine your overall level

After completing the Quick Scan, you have a level for each of the six
dimensions. Your **overall literacy level** is not the average — it is
the **dominant pattern**.

Look at where the weight of your placements sits:

- **If 4 or more dimensions are at the same level**, that is your dominant
  level. The remaining dimensions are either growth edges (higher) or gaps
  (lower).
- **If your dimensions are split across two adjacent levels** (e.g., three
  at Level 2 and three at Level 3), your dominant level is the lower one
  with the higher one emerging. Example: "Solidly Level 2 with Level 3
  emerging."
- **If you have a wide spread** (e.g., Level 1 in some dimensions and
  Level 3 in others), report the range and investigate — a wide spread
  often indicates that some self-ratings need refinement through Guided
  Exploration.

## What confidence means

| Phases completed | Confidence | What it tells you |
| --------------- | ---------- | ----------------- |
| Quick Scan only | Low | A starting point. Your self-rating has not been tested. Useful for initial orientation and team aggregation. |
| Quick Scan + Guided Exploration | Medium | Refined. A facilitator or structured self-reflection has probed your placements. Reliable enough for development planning. |
| All three phases | High | Validated. Self-rating, conversational probing, and scenario/evidence review all inform the placement. Reliable for instructor placement and team-level decisions. |

## Common profile patterns

**The Generalist** — all dimensions within one level of each other. This
person has developed broadly. Development focus: go deeper in whichever
dimension is most relevant to their current platform challenges.

**The Technical-Human Split** — strong on Context Awareness, Pattern
Application, and Strategic Thinking; weaker on Cognitive Load Design,
Feedback and Measurement, and Human and Cultural Dimensions (or vice
versa). Development focus: the weaker cluster.

**The Thinker-Doer Split** — strong on exploration and design dimensions
(Levels 0-2 skills); weaker on engineering and stewardship dimensions
(Levels 3-4 skills). This person may be in a role that limits their
opportunity to ship and measure. Development focus: find opportunities
to close the loop.

**The Spiky Expert** — one or two dimensions significantly ahead of the
rest. Common in specialists (e.g., a metrics expert at Level 3 on Feedback
and Measurement but Level 1 elsewhere). Development focus: the lowest
dimension, as it is likely limiting overall effectiveness.

## When to reassess

- **After completing a learning cycle** — you have worked through one or more
  course modules and practised the associated fluency habits
- **After a significant platform initiative** — you have shipped, measured,
  and iterated on a major platform change
- **Quarterly, at most** — literacy changes through practice, not study.
  More frequent assessment measures noise, not signal.
- **When your role changes** — a move from design to strategy, or from
  individual contribution to team leadership, may shift your profile.
